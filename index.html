Results

What I did at a 350-person bank

  • Pitched the AI strategy to the CEO and the full executive team, then drafted the board-ready AI strategy.
  • Wrote the company's first ChatGPT guidance in 2023, then its Gen AI policy with permitted, restricted and prohibited uses.
  • Negotiated a vendor-funded AI discovery at no cash cost, under a hard 127-hour cap.
  • Put a knowledge base built from past calls into the contact center, which reps search while on a call.
