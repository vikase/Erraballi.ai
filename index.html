Results

What I did at Heritage Bank of Commerce

  • Pitched the AI strategy to the CEO and the full executive team, then drafted the board-ready AI strategy.
  • Wrote the bank's first ChatGPT guidance in 2023, then its Gen AI policy and a NIST AI RMF-based framework with permitted, restricted and prohibited tiers.
  • Set a rule that AI could never downgrade a vendor rated High or Critical risk, and that every change needed a written reason.
  • Grew Teams from under 20 to about 285 active users in a year, under guardrails written with the CRO and the Chief Compliance Officer.
  • Cut complaint intake from 45 to 13 minutes per complaint, with every complaint captured and a full audit trail.
  • Built contract-clause triage that quoted contracts word for word, because the General Counsel did not trust summaries.
