What working with me is like

Your business, in good hands.

  • I start with your work, not the technology. The first days are with your people and how the work really gets done.
  • I build the first version myself. You see it working in weeks, not described in a report.
  • You know the cost before we start. Written scope, fixed fee. No open-ended bills.
  • Your data stays yours. Approved tools only, and customer data never goes into public AI. I held that line inside a regulated bank.
  • Your people come with you. I train them and leave champions behind, so it keeps working after I leave.
  • Three gates: validate, pilot, scale. If an idea is not worth the money, I will tell you at the first one.
